Abe Nito

Hello, I’m Abe.

A Product Designer at Microsoft, based in Vancouver, BC, currently shaping Azure portal experiences across Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS), Compute, end-to-end experiences, and Growth.

I thrive in complex problem spaces, turning technical challenges into simple, intuitive, and meaningful experiences. My approach to design goes beyond the interface — thinking in systems, connecting the dots across experiences, and balancing customer needs, technical constraints, and business goals.

Born and raised in Manila, Philippines, I began my career in marketing and advertising before moving to Vancouver and finding my way into tech. That background continues to shape how I approach design today, from understanding human behavior and communicating meaningful stories to influencing strategy and bringing people together around a shared vision.

At the heart of my work is a curiosity about people and technology, and a belief that even the most complex products can feel approachable and human.

Outside of design, you’ll find me exploring new places, discovering new tastes, seeking out good coffee, and enjoying live music. I love working with my hands through pottery, staying active through cycling and running, and, more recently, seeing the world through the lens of a camera.

Always curious, always creating, and always discovering something new.

Kamusta? — “How are you?” in Tagalog. A familiar greeting from the place that raised me.
